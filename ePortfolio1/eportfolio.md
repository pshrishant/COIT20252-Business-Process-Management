# COIT20252 – e-Portfolio 1: Process Analysis

## 1. What Business Analysis Is and Why It Matters

![YouTube](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pshrishant/COIT20252-Business-Process-Management/main/ePortfolio1/Images/youtube.png)

**Artefact 1: YouTube Video** — [Business Analysis Explained in Under 10 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=STVRW9UzS48)

This video explains what a business analyst does, framed around PMI's project environment. Author breaks the role down into three parts: elicit stakeholder requirements, ensure the solution is congruent with the requirements, and confirm the solution had the desired impact such as more revenue or usability. He then provides examples of specific tools that can be used for every step: stakeholder registers and classification matrices to help identify who you need to involve; the nominal group technique and affinity diagrams to help elicit requirements; process mapping and context diagrams to visualize requirements; requirement traceability matrices, MoSCoW prioritisation and acceptance criteria to validate the solution.

I selected this artefact because it highlighted a difference between business analysis, eliciting and validating requirements across a whole project, as compared to generic understanding of business process.
